# ILOVENEAT Fashion House

Design and build a world-class, multi-page luxury fashion website for **ILOVENEAT (Instagram @iloveneat.co)**. Study the brand’s Instagram for its actual identity, clothing, styling, photography, and aesthetic. Do not invent a different brand direction.

**Creative direction:** Prada-level luxury editorial, contemporary fashion-house art direction, bold typography, refined whitespace, unconventional layouts, premium imagery, and cinematic storytelling. The result must feel like a $20,000 custom-designed website, never a generic AI-generated ecommerce template.

**Homepage:** Use my supplied video as the full-screen hero, edge-to-edge, with autoplay, muted, loop, and cover. Create immersive scroll transitions, subtle parallax, oversized editorial typography, animated outfit cards, asymmetric fashion grids, and a cinematic collection showcase. Product cards should reveal alternate outfit imagery on hover, with smooth image transitions and refined interactions.

**Pages:** Home, Shop, Collections, Product Details, Our Story, and Contact. Include a functional shopping experience with product variants, cart, and checkout flow. Every page must feel art-directed and visually consistent.

**Animations:** Smooth Lenis scrolling, GSAP ScrollTrigger, pinned editorial sections, staggered image reveals, fluid transitions, and sophisticated micro-interactions. Motion should feel intentional and expensive, never excessive.

**Design rules:** No numbered sections, no hyphenated section headings, no generic gradients, floating blobs, excessive rounded cards, or typical AI layouts. Avoid template aesthetics. Use the brand’s authentic visual identity, premium typography, strong composition, and exceptional attention to detail.

Build responsive desktop and mobile experiences. Prioritize visual excellence, performance, usability, and a truly bespoke luxury fashion experience.
